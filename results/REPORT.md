## Metric

```text
center_up       = normalize(front_up + rear_up)
U               = 1 - whole_body_tilt / pi
A               = 1 - relative_front_rear_angle / pi
whole_cat_score = U * A
```

The score ranges from 0 to 1. It ignores common yaw, penalizes relative spine twist, and reaches 1 only when the whole-body centerline is upright and the front/rear frames are aligned. The rear body is reconstructed from the front IMU and the spine encoders.

## Limitations

- Tail/no-tail assignment was not randomized or interleaved within collection session; date and morphology are partly confounded.
- The metric was developed after viewing these data. Treat the p-values as exploratory.
- Per-trial provenance (which policy flew each drop, file date labels) is in the 'Data notes' of data/README.md and in data/hardware_trials/MANIFEST.csv. Date is a model term and defines the permutation blocks.
